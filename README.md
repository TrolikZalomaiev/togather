# Together: Browse & Watch in Sync

A Chrome extension that turns two browsers into one. Open a website with someone far away and see the same thing at the same time, as if you were sitting side by side.

Nothing is streamed: each browser loads the page and the video itself, so everyone gets full quality on their own screen, with their own sound.

## What stays in sync

- Links and pages: open a page and it opens for your partner too
- Scrolling
- Cursors, labeled with each person's name
- Clicks on buttons, tabs, filters and drop-down lists
- Search boxes and other ordinary text fields
- Video: play, pause, seeking and speed, with automatic drift correction

It works both ways: everyone in the room is equal.

## How to use

1. Click the Together icon and choose **Create a room**.
2. Send the 6-character room code to your partner.
3. Your partner enters the code and clicks **Join**.

Only the current tab is synced. Your other tabs stay private.

## Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Data goes directly between your browsers over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection. Passwords, payment card fields and one-time codes are never shared.
